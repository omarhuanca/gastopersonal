## Full description (max 4000)
Gastos Personales is a simple way to keep track of where your money goes. Log each expense in seconds, then see how much you have spent in each category.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Log expenses with a description, date, amount, currency and category.
• Dictate the description with your voice instead of typing.
• Create your own categories and currencies on the spot, right from the form. BOB, USD and a General category come ready to use.
• Edit or delete an expense whenever you need to (you are asked to confirm before anything is deleted).
• Open the "Group by category" report: pick a date range and see each category's total and how many expenses it has.
• If you use several currencies, totals are shown separately for each one, never mixed together.
• Open a category's detail to see its expenses in the chosen period.
• Share an expense, or a category's detail, as an image through the app you prefer.

BUILT TO STAY SIMPLE
• Works offline and needs no account.
• No ads.
• Your data is stored on your phone.
• Available in Spanish and English; switch the language from the menu.
• Light and dark theme, following your phone's setting.

Gastos Personales asks for no special permissions and does not send your expenses to any server.
